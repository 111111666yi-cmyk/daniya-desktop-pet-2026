- Relationship reset protection: confirmed blocking safety is in place. The UI shows a confirmation dialog before reset, creates a timestamped backup under `backups/relationship_state_reset/`, refuses reset when backup fails, and does not touch `data/affinity.json`, `data/chat_history.jsonl`, `data/reminders.json`, or `data/notes.txt`.
